Digital Workflow Engine

GIS & Geo-tagging
GeoServer & Leaflet-powered interactive maps with geo-tagged land parcels, cadastral boundaries, and spatial analytics.

Real-Time Dashboards
Project-wise, district-wise, state-wise, and national-level analytics with KPIs, heatmaps, and predictive indicators.

API Integration
RESTful API integration with DILRMP land records, cadastral maps, PFMS financial systems, and state government portals.

Automated Alerts

Document Repository

Role-Based Access
Granular RBAC for Ministry, State, District, and Field levels with audit logs and GoI cybersecurity compliance.

AI & Predictive Analytics
ML-powered timeline prediction, bottleneck detection, compensation trend analysis, and policy formulation support.
